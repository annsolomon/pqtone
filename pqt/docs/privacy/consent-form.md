**Project:** PQT, software that counts how many people are waiting in a queue so a shop can open a till
sooner. It does **not** recognise faces or identify anyone.

**Recording:** [date], [start time]–[end time], at [shop name and address], by [data owner's name].

**Premises owner:** [name], who has agreed to this recording in writing.

### What will happen

1. One to three fixed cameras will record video (no audio) of the checkout area and one aisle.
2. The video is used only to test and improve how accurately the software counts people.
3. The video is stored on the recording device's encrypted disk. For processing it may also be copied to
   **Google Drive and Google Colab** (Google's cloud services) and, only if those are unavailable, to
   **E2E Networks** (a cloud provider in India). It goes nowhere else and is never published.
4. The video is deleted once testing is complete, and at most **180 days** after the recording date.
5. Numbers derived from the video (for example "4 people in the queue at 10:32") may be kept and published;
   they cannot identify you.

### Your choices

- Taking part is voluntary. You can stop at any time during the recording by telling [name].
- You can withdraw later: email [contact email] with the date and approximate time you were recorded. Your
  footage will be deleted from every location above within 7 days.
- Saying no has no consequence for you.
